## Scene 1: Cosmic Void, Smooth Mars Approach & Title Reveal [COMPLETED]

* **0.0s – 4.5s:** Deep starfield, celestial silence, distant solar flare.
* **4.5s – 13.0s:** Sunlit Mars enters frame; Endurance visible as microscopic rotating speck.
